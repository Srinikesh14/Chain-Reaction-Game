HOW TO PLAY

  • Turn 1: You must click an EMPTY cell.
  • Next Turns: You can only click YOUR OWN cells to add 1 orb.
  • Capacity: Corners hold 1, Edges hold 2, Center cells hold 3.
  • Explosions: Hitting capacity causes the cell to explode!
  • Hacker Portals: Cyan cells warp orbs to the Black Hole.
  • The Time Bomb: Detonates after 1 round, firing an EMP cross.
  • Time Machine: Use the Left/Right arrows to rewrite history during the game, or replay it after!
